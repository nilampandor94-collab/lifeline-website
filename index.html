Why blood donation matters

Blood cannot be made in a lab. It can only come from people who choose to give. Hospitals need a steady supply for surgeries, accidents, cancer care and mothers in childbirth.

❤️

Saves lives

A single unit is separated into red cells, plasma and platelets, so it can help up to three patients.

⏱️

Quick and safe

The donation itself takes about 10 minutes. New, sterile equipment is used for every donor.

🩺

Free health check

Before donating you get your pulse, blood pressure, temperature and haemoglobin checked.

🔁

Your body recovers

The body replaces the fluid within hours and the red cells within a few weeks.

Basic donor rules

• Age 18 to 65 years

• Weight at least 50 kg

• Haemoglobin at least 12.5 g/dL

• At least 3 months since the last donation

• No fever, infection or recent tattoo or surgery
